# AI Wardrobe

Turn your closet into an AI stylist.

Upload your clothes.
Get personalized outfits instantly.

![demo](./demo.gif)

---

## ✨ Features

* AI Outfit Recommendations
* Personal Style DNA Analysis
* Smart Wardrobe Upload
* Modern AI-Native UI
* Minimal Techwear & Clean Fit Styling
* DeepSeek-Powered Fashion Assistant

---

## 🧠 Why

Most people don't need more clothes.

They need less decision fatigue.

AI Wardrobe helps users decide what to wear every day using AI-generated outfit recommendations and personalized style analysis.

The goal is simple:

> Reduce the mental friction of getting dressed.

---

## 🪞 Style DNA

AI Wardrobe doesn't just recommend outfits.

It understands your vibe.

Example:

```text
Style DNA:
- Tokyo Minimalist
- Scandinavian Neutral
- Clean Techwear
```
